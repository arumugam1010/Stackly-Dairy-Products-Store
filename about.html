Continues History

Our cows are pasture fed and produce pure, unadulterated organic milk.

Founded in 1982 nestled across rolling green hills, Hancock Farm began with just twelve heritage Jersey cows and an unwavering commitment to pure, chemical-free dairy. Over four decades, our family farm has blossomed into an award-winning organic sanctuary where animal wellness, fertile soil, and traditional craftsmanship come first. Every sunrise begins with respect for our herd and pride in the honest milk we bottle.

Our Mission

To deliver the cleanest, most nutrient-dense whole dairy directly from our pesticide-free pastures to family tables, preserving human health and honoring nature's balance.

We are relentlessly dedicated to zero synthetic hormones (rBST-free), non-GMO feed, and low-stress humane living conditions for every single cow in our care.

Our Vision

To pioneer regenerative agriculture across the country, showing that world-class dairy production can actively restore grasslands, enrich biodiversity, and support local farming communities.

Through innovative solar technology, circular glass bottling, and open educational farm tours, we aspire to inspire the next generation of conscious agricultural stewardship.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find transparent, honest answers regarding our pasture feeding methods, milk processing standards, doorstep delivery, and farm visits.
What do Hancock Farm cows eat throughout the year?
Our cows graze on fresh certified organic clover, orchard grass, and wild chicory from early spring through late autumn. During colder winter months, they feed on certified organic fermented silage and organic alfalfa hay harvested directly from our own certified fields. We never feed corn by-products, soy fillers, or genetically modified concentrates.
Why do you bottle your organic milk in glass bottles instead of plastic?
Glass bottles maintain a colder, consistent temperature that preserves the sweet, delicate flavor of farm milk without any chemical plastic leaching or microplastics. Furthermore, our returnable glass bottle program is 100% circular: customers simply return rinsed bottles for reuse, preventing hundreds of thousands of single-use plastic jugs from entering landfills.
What is the difference between vat pasteurization and ultra-pasteurization?
Ultra-pasteurized commercial milk is heated to extreme temperatures (over 280°F) to give it a months-long grocery shelf life, which alters proteins and neutralizes natural enzymes. At Hancock Farm, we practice gentle low-temperature vat pasteurization (145°F for 30 minutes). This eliminates harmful bacteria while safeguarding healthy digestive enzymes, natural vitamins, and the authentic sweet cream flavor.
Can families and school groups visit the farm for a tour?
Yes! We warmly invite families, students, and community members to our Open Farm Days held every Saturday and Sunday. Visitors can take guided pasture wagon rides, watch the morning milking process, bottle-feed our friendly calves, and enjoy hands-on butter churning workshops at our education barn. Reservations can be made online or by calling us.
How does your weekly home delivery and glass bottle exchange work?
Our refrigerated delivery vans make scheduled doorstep drops twice weekly. Simply leave your rinsed empty glass bottles inside your insulated Hancock doorstep porch cooler on delivery morning. Our driver places your fresh cold milk, butter, or cheese inside and picks up the empties, crediting your account automatically for each returned bottle.
