Travel & Logistics

Getting to Hancock Farm: Directions & Parking

Located just 45 minutes from the metro center nestled among rolling green pasturelands with smooth paved highway access and abundant parking.

By Car & Highway Routes

Take Highway 28 North to Exit 14 towards Greenville Valley. Follow scenic Farmstead Road for 3 miles until you see the historic red barn on your right.

150+ free parking spaces & 4 EV charging bays

Public Transit & Shuttle

Take the regional Valley Commuter Train to Greenville Central Station. Our complimentary electric farm shuttle van departs hourly on Saturdays and Sundays.

Free weekend shuttle 9:30 AM – 4:30 PM

Accessibility & Pets

Paved accessible ramps connect the main barn, restrooms, and country store. Friendly leashed dogs are warmly welcomed on outdoor orchard nature trails.

Stroller and wheelchair accessible throughout

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions for Visitors

Common questions regarding weekend visits, farm country store purchases, pet policies, and private event bookings.
Do I need advance tickets or reservations to visit the farm?
No tickets or reservations are required for general admission! Visiting our Farm Country Store, picnic meadow, and viewing the calves from the fence line is 100% free and open to everyone. If you wish to join specialized cheese making classes or large school group tours, advance booking online is recommended.
Can I purchase cold glass milk bottles and cheeses directly on site?
Yes! Our on-site Farm Country Store is open seven days a week from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We stock our complete assortment of fresh organic whole milk in returnable glass bottles, heavy clotted cream, aged farmhouse cheeses, cultured butter, raw wildflower honey, and artisan ice cream scoops.
Are dogs and family pets allowed on the farm grounds?
Well-behaved, leashed dogs are warmly welcomed on our outdoor orchard trails and grassy picnic areas. To maintain bio-security and animal welfare standards, pets are not allowed inside active dairy milking barns, cheese processing areas, or calf nurseries.
How do I schedule a wedding or private event in your historic barn?
Our 1920s timber barn accommodates up to 220 guests for weddings, farm-to-table dinners, and corporate gatherings. Please contact our events director directly at events@hancockfarm.com or call (1800) 456 7894 to schedule an on-site walkthrough.
