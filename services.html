What We Do

At Hancock Farm, we provide comprehensive agricultural and dairy management services backed by four decades of organic pedigree breeding, veterinary excellence, and sustainable pasture engineering.

Working on Best Breeding Processes Of Cows

We utilize genetic lineage mapping and natural breeding techniques to preserve heritage Jersey and Guernsey bloodlines, ensuring high butterfat production, disease resilience, and calm herd temperament.

Selecting The Best Cows for getting More Milk

Advanced somatic cell and nutritional monitoring identifies top-performing milkers. Our selection criteria prioritize animal comfort, longevity, and superior A2 beta-casein protein yield.

Milk Preservation In Farm

Closed-loop stainless steel chilling systems drop milk temperature to 38°F within 15 minutes of collection, halting bacterial proliferation and preserving raw flavor enzymes naturally.

Automated Milking for High Production of Milk

Voluntary robotic milking parlors allow cows to choose their own milking schedule. Teat-massaging cups and real-time milk sensors eliminate animal stress while ensuring highest hygiene.
